## Always say the subject (A1)

En inglés casi todas las frases llevan sujeto, incluso cuando en español lo omitimos.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: omitting_subject.

### El sujeto no se omite

En español decimos “Es importante” o “Llueve”. En inglés hace falta un sujeto: usa it, there o el pronombre que corresponda.

- It is important. — _Es importante._
- It rains a lot here. — _Llueve mucho aquí._
- He works in a bank. — _Trabaja en un banco._

### There is / There are para “hay”

Para decir que algo existe o está presente usa there is (singular) o there are (plural).

- There is a café near here. — _Hay un café cerca de aquí._
- There are two bedrooms. — _Hay dos habitaciones._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ Is possible to pay by card. → ✔ It is possible to pay by card.. Falta el sujeto “it”.
- ✗ Is a nice city. → ✔ It is a nice city.. El verbo “is” necesita sujeto.
- ✗ Have two bedrooms. → ✔ There are two bedrooms.. Para “hay” se usa there is / there are.

**Quiz**

- ¿Cuál es correcta para “Es muy tarde”?
  - ✔ It is very late.
  - ✗ Is very late.
  - ✗ Very late is.
- ¿Cuál es correcta para “Hay una farmacia aquí cerca”?
  - ✔ There is a pharmacy near here.
  - ✗ Has a pharmacy near here.
  - ✗ Is a pharmacy near here.
- ¿Cuál es correcta para “Llueve mucho”?
  - ✔ It rains a lot.
  - ✗ Rains a lot.
  - ✗ Is raining a lot it.
- ¿Cuál es correcta para “Trabaja los sábados”?
  - ✔ She works on Saturdays.
  - ✗ Works on Saturdays.
  - ✗ Is work on Saturdays.

## want / need / like + to + verb (A1)

Tras want, need, would like, hope, decide… el verbo siguiente lleva “to”.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: missing_to.

### Verbo + to + infinitivo

Después de want, need, would like, hope, plan, decide y try, el segundo verbo va con “to”.

- I want to learn English. — _Quiero aprender inglés._
- She needs to leave early. — _Ella necesita irse temprano._
- We decided to stay home. — _Decidimos quedarnos en casa._

### Con can, must, should NO va “to”

Los verbos modales (can, could, must, should, will) van seguidos del verbo sin “to”.

- I can swim. — _Sé nadar._
- You should rest. — _Deberías descansar._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I need learn more words. → ✔ I need to learn more words.. need + to + verbo.
- ✗ I want go home. → ✔ I want to go home.. want + to + verbo.
- ✗ I can to swim. → ✔ I can swim.. Tras “can” no se usa “to”.

**Quiz**

- “Quiero comprar un billete.”
  - ✔ I want to buy a ticket.
  - ✗ I want buy a ticket.
  - ✗ I want buying a ticket.
- “Necesito hablar contigo.”
  - ✔ I need to talk to you.
  - ✗ I need talk to you.
  - ✗ I need to talking you.
- “Puedes entrar.”
  - ✔ You can come in.
  - ✗ You can to come in.
  - ✗ You can coming in.
- “Esperamos verte pronto.”
  - ✔ We hope to see you soon.
  - ✗ We hope see you soon.
  - ✗ We hope seeing you soon.

## Verbs with prepositions (A2)

Muchos verbos van con una preposición fija que no siempre coincide con el español.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: missing_to_listen, preposition.

### listen to, look at, wait for

En español “escuchar música” y “esperar el autobús” no llevan preposición. En inglés sí: listen to, wait for, look at, look for.

- I listen to music every day. — _Escucho música todos los días._
- We are waiting for the bus. — _Estamos esperando el autobús._
- Look at this photo. — _Mira esta foto._
- I'm looking for my keys. — _Estoy buscando mis llaves._

### Otros que conviene memorizar

depend on (depender de), arrive in/at (llegar a), think about (pensar en), talk to (hablar con), agree with (estar de acuerdo con).

- It depends on the weather. — _Depende del tiempo._
- We arrived in Madrid at noon. — _Llegamos a Madrid al mediodía._
- I agree with you. — _Estoy de acuerdo contigo._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I listen music. → ✔ I listen to music.. listen siempre va con “to”.
- ✗ I'm waiting the bus. → ✔ I'm waiting for the bus.. wait + for.
- ✗ I'm agree with you. → ✔ I agree with you.. agree es un verbo; no se usa “be”.

**Quiz**

- “Estoy buscando mi pasaporte.”
  - ✔ I'm looking for my passport.
  - ✗ I'm looking my passport.
  - ✗ I'm looking at my passport for.
- “Escucho podcasts en el tren.”
  - ✔ I listen to podcasts on the train.
  - ✗ I listen podcasts on the train.
  - ✗ I listen at podcasts on the train.
- “Depende de ti.”
  - ✔ It depends on you.
  - ✗ It depends of you.
  - ✗ It depends you.
- “Estoy de acuerdo.”
  - ✔ I agree.
  - ✗ I am agree.
  - ✗ I have agree.

## Adjectives never take -s (A1)

El adjetivo en inglés no cambia con el plural ni con el género, y va antes del sustantivo.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: adjective_plural.

### Sin plural, sin género

Solo el sustantivo se pluraliza. El adjetivo se queda igual: a big house, two big houses.

- They are tall students. — _Son estudiantes altos._
- She has green eyes. — _Ella tiene ojos verdes._

### Antes del sustantivo

El adjetivo va antes del sustantivo (al revés que en español): a red car, un coche rojo.

- I live in a small flat. — _Vivo en un piso pequeño._
- We saw an interesting film. — _Vimos una película interesante._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ They are differents. → ✔ They are different.. Los adjetivos no llevan -s.
- ✗ I have a car red. → ✔ I have a red car.. El adjetivo va antes del sustantivo.
- ✗ Two importants things. → ✔ Two important things.. important no se pluraliza.

**Quiz**

- “Tienen coches rápidos.”
  - ✔ They have fast cars.
  - ✗ They have fasts cars.
  - ✗ They have cars fast.
- “Son muy simpáticos.”
  - ✔ They are very nice.
  - ✗ They are very nices.
  - ✗ They are very nice ones.
- “Una casa grande.”
  - ✔ A big house.
  - ✗ A house big.
  - ✗ A bigs house.
- “Dos problemas importantes.”
  - ✔ Two important problems.
  - ✗ Two importants problems.
  - ✗ Two problems importants.

## False friends (falsos amigos) (A2)

Palabras que se parecen al español pero significan otra cosa.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: false_friend.

### Los más traicioneros

Estas palabras parecen españolas, pero no lo son.

- actually = en realidad (no “actualmente”: currently) — _Actually, I prefer tea. / Currently, I live in Madrid._
- embarrassed = avergonzado (no “embarazada”: pregnant) — _I was embarrassed. / She is pregnant._
- library = biblioteca (no “librería”: bookshop) — _I borrow books from the library._
- sensible = sensato (no “sensible”: sensitive) — _That is a sensible idea._
- assist = ayudar (no “asistir a”: attend) — _I attend classes on Monday._
- realise = darse cuenta (no “realizar”: carry out) — _I realised my mistake._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ Actually I live in Madrid. → ✔ Currently I live in Madrid.. “Actually” significa “en realidad”.
- ✗ I was very embarrassed (para decir “embarazada”). → ✔ She is pregnant.. embarrassed = avergonzado.
- ✗ I assist the English class. → ✔ I attend the English class.. assist = ayudar.

**Quiz**

- “Actualmente vivo en Barcelona.”
  - ✔ Currently, I live in Barcelona.
  - ✗ Actually, I live in Barcelona.
  - ✗ Nowadays I am living actually in Barcelona.
- “Voy a clase todos los lunes.” (asistir)
  - ✔ I attend class every Monday.
  - ✗ I assist class every Monday.
  - ✗ I assist to class every Monday.
- “Compré un libro en la librería.”
  - ✔ I bought a book at the bookshop.
  - ✗ I bought a book at the library.
  - ✗ I bought a book at the librery.
- “Me dio vergüenza.”
  - ✔ I was embarrassed.
  - ✗ I was embarrased pregnant.
  - ✗ I felt embarazada.

## have, take, make, do (A2)

Cuatro verbos muy comunes que forman expresiones fijas.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: meal_verb.

### have para comidas

Las comidas y bebidas se “tienen”: have breakfast, have lunch, have a coffee.

- I have breakfast at eight. — _Desayuno a las ocho._
- Let's have a coffee. — _Tomemos un café._

### take, make y do

take a shower / a photo / a break; make a decision / a mistake / a plan; do homework / the dishes / a job.

- I take a shower every morning. — _Me ducho cada mañana._
- She made a mistake. — _Ella cometió un error._
- He does his homework after school. — _Hace los deberes después de clase._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I make breakfast at eight. → ✔ I have breakfast at eight.. Para tomar una comida se usa have (make = prepararla).
- ✗ I do a lot of mistakes. → ✔ I make a lot of mistakes.. mistake va con make.
- ✗ I make my homework. → ✔ I do my homework.. homework va con do.

**Quiz**

- “Cometí un error.”
  - ✔ I made a mistake.
  - ✗ I did a mistake.
  - ✗ I had a mistake.
- “Hago los deberes por la tarde.”
  - ✔ I do my homework in the afternoon.
  - ✗ I make my homework in the afternoon.
  - ✗ I take my homework in the afternoon.
- “Almuerzo a las dos.”
  - ✔ I have lunch at two.
  - ✗ I make lunch at two.
  - ✗ I eat the lunch at two o'clock of.
- “Voy a darme una ducha.”
  - ✔ I'm going to take a shower.
  - ✗ I'm going to make a shower.
  - ✗ I'm going to do a shower.

## Word order in statements & questions (A2)

El orden en inglés es fijo: sujeto + verbo + objeto, y las preguntas usan un auxiliar.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: word_order.

### Afirmaciones: sujeto + verbo + objeto

No se puede mover el sujeto al final como en español (“Llegó Ana”).

- Ana arrived late. — _Llegó Ana tarde._
- I like this city a lot. — _Me gusta mucho esta ciudad._

### Preguntas: palabra interrogativa + auxiliar + sujeto + verbo

En las preguntas hace falta do/does/did (o be/can/will) antes del sujeto.

- Where do you live? — _¿Dónde vives?_
- What time does the train leave? — _¿A qué hora sale el tren?_
- Can you help me? — _¿Puedes ayudarme?_

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ Where you live? → ✔ Where do you live?. Falta el auxiliar do.
- ✗ Arrived Ana late. → ✔ Ana arrived late.. El sujeto va primero.
- ✗ What means this word? → ✔ What does this word mean?. Auxiliar + sujeto + verbo.

**Quiz**

- “¿Dónde trabajas?”
  - ✔ Where do you work?
  - ✗ Where you work?
  - ✗ Where work you?
- “¿Qué significa esta palabra?”
  - ✔ What does this word mean?
  - ✗ What means this word?
  - ✗ What this word means?
- “¿A qué hora empieza la película?”
  - ✔ What time does the film start?
  - ✗ What time starts the film?
  - ✗ What time the film starts?
- “Me encanta la comida italiana.”
  - ✔ I love Italian food.
  - ✗ I love food Italian.
  - ✗ Love I Italian food.

## Past simple (A2)

Para acciones terminadas en un momento del pasado: -ed en los regulares y formas propias en los irregulares.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: verb_tense.

### Verbos regulares: -ed

Añade -ed: work → worked, visit → visited. En negativas y preguntas se usa did + verbo base.

- I visited my aunt yesterday. — _Visité a mi tía ayer._
- Did you work last Sunday? — _¿Trabajaste el domingo pasado?_
- She didn't call me. — _Ella no me llamó._

### Irregulares frecuentes

go → went, have → had, see → saw, eat → ate, buy → bought, make → made, take → took.

- Last weekend I went to the beach. — _El fin de semana pasado fui a la playa._
- We ate pizza. — _Comimos pizza._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ Yesterday I go to the cinema. → ✔ Yesterday I went to the cinema.. Con “yesterday” va el pasado.
- ✗ We eated pizza. → ✔ We ate pizza.. eat es irregular: ate.
- ✗ Did you went there? → ✔ Did you go there?. Tras did va el verbo base.

**Quiz**

- “Ayer compré un abrigo.”
  - ✔ Yesterday I bought a coat.
  - ✗ Yesterday I buyed a coat.
  - ✗ Yesterday I buy a coat.
- “¿Viste la película?”
  - ✔ Did you see the film?
  - ✗ Did you saw the film?
  - ✗ You saw the film?
- “No fui a trabajar.”
  - ✔ I didn't go to work.
  - ✗ I didn't went to work.
  - ✗ I no went to work.
- “Cenamos tarde.”
  - ✔ We had dinner late.
  - ✗ We have dinner late yesterday.
  - ✗ We haved dinner late.

## Present perfect vs past simple (B1)

Present perfect para experiencias y situaciones que conectan con el presente; past simple para un momento concreto.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: verb_tense.

### have/has + participio

Úsalo para experiencias (ever, never), cambios recientes y cosas que duran hasta ahora (for, since).

- I have been to London twice. — _He estado en Londres dos veces._
- She has lived here for five years. — _Ella vive aquí desde hace cinco años._
- Have you ever tried sushi? — _¿Alguna vez has probado el sushi?_

### Con un momento concreto, past simple

Si dices cuándo ocurrió (yesterday, last week, in 2020), usa past simple.

- I went to London last year. — _Fui a Londres el año pasado._
- We met in 2019. — _Nos conocimos en 2019._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I live here since 2020. → ✔ I have lived here since 2020.. since/for + situación que continúa: present perfect.
- ✗ I have seen him yesterday. → ✔ I saw him yesterday.. Con “yesterday” se usa past simple.
- ✗ Did you ever go to Italy? → ✔ Have you ever been to Italy?. Experiencias de vida: present perfect.

**Quiz**

- “Vivo aquí desde 2018.”
  - ✔ I have lived here since 2018.
  - ✗ I live here since 2018.
  - ✗ I am living here since 2018.
- “Lo vi ayer.”
  - ✔ I saw him yesterday.
  - ✗ I have seen him yesterday.
  - ✗ I have saw him yesterday.
- “¿Has estado alguna vez en Roma?”
  - ✔ Have you ever been to Rome?
  - ✗ Did you ever be to Rome?
  - ✗ Are you ever been to Rome?
- “No he terminado todavía.”
  - ✔ I haven't finished yet.
  - ✗ I didn't finished yet.
  - ✗ I don't have finished yet.

## in / on / at (time and place) (A2)

Tres preposiciones, una lógica: de lo más amplio (in) a lo más puntual (at).

Ayuda a corregir los errores: preposition.

### Tiempo

in + meses, años, estaciones, partes del día (in May, in 2024, in the morning). on + días y fechas (on Monday, on 5 May). at + horas y momentos (at 7 o'clock, at night, at the weekend en inglés británico).

- My birthday is in May. — _Mi cumpleaños es en mayo._
- The meeting is on Monday. — _La reunión es el lunes._
- I wake up at seven. — _Me despierto a las siete._

### Lugar

in + espacios cerrados o ciudades (in the room, in Madrid). on + superficies (on the table). at + puntos concretos (at the station, at home, at work).

- She lives in Valencia. — _Vive en Valencia._
- Your phone is on the table. — _Tu móvil está sobre la mesa._
- I'll wait for you at the station. — _Te espero en la estación._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I'll see you in Monday. → ✔ I'll see you on Monday.. Los días llevan on.
- ✗ I arrive on the airport at 5. → ✔ I arrive at the airport at 5.. Un punto concreto: at.
- ✗ I was born at 1995. → ✔ I was born in 1995.. Los años llevan in.

**Quiz**

- “Nos vemos el viernes.”
  - ✔ See you on Friday.
  - ✗ See you in Friday.
  - ✗ See you at Friday.
- “Nací en 1998.”
  - ✔ I was born in 1998.
  - ✗ I was born on 1998.
  - ✗ I was born at 1998.
- “Estoy en casa.”
  - ✔ I'm at home.
  - ✗ I'm in home.
  - ✗ I'm on home.
- “La clase empieza a las nueve.”
  - ✔ The class starts at nine.
  - ✗ The class starts in nine.
  - ✗ The class starts on nine.

## a / an / the / no article (A2)

Cuándo usar a/an, cuándo the y cuándo ningún artículo.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: article.

### a / an: algo nuevo o uno cualquiera

Usa a antes de sonido consonante y an antes de sonido vocal. Presenta algo por primera vez o habla de un ejemplo cualquiera.

- I have a dog and an umbrella. — _Tengo un perro y un paraguas._
- She is an engineer. — _Ella es ingeniera._

### the: algo ya conocido o único

Usa the cuando quien escucha sabe de qué hablas, o es único (the sun, the Internet).

- I saw a film. The film was great. — _Vi una película. La película fue genial._
- Close the door, please. — _Cierra la puerta, por favor._

### Sin artículo

No se usa artículo para hablar en general en plural o incontables (I love music), ni con la mayoría de ciudades y países.

- I love music. — _Me encanta la música._
- Life is beautiful. — _La vida es bella._
- I live in Spain. — _Vivo en España._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ I love the music. → ✔ I love music.. En general no lleva artículo.
- ✗ She is teacher. → ✔ She is a teacher.. Las profesiones llevan a/an.
- ✗ I have an umbrella of black. → ✔ I have a black umbrella.. Antes de “black” suena consonante: a.

**Quiz**

- “Soy médico.”
  - ✔ I'm a doctor.
  - ✗ I'm doctor.
  - ✗ I'm the doctor.
- “Me gusta el café.” (en general)
  - ✔ I like coffee.
  - ✗ I like the coffee.
  - ✗ I like a coffee.
- “Necesito un paraguas.”
  - ✔ I need an umbrella.
  - ✗ I need a umbrella.
  - ✗ I need umbrella.
- “Cierra la ventana.”
  - ✔ Close the window.
  - ✗ Close window.
  - ✗ Close a window.

## can, could, should, must (B1)

Los verbos modales: habilidad, permiso, consejo y obligación. Siempre seguidos del verbo base.

Ayuda a corregir los errores: verb_tense, missing_to.

### Qué expresa cada uno

can/could = habilidad o permiso (could es más cortés); should = consejo; must = obligación fuerte; have to = obligación externa.

- Could you open the window? — _¿Podrías abrir la ventana?_
- You should see a doctor. — _Deberías ir al médico._
- You must wear a seatbelt. — _Debes llevar el cinturón._

### Sin -s, sin to

Los modales no añaden -s en he/she/it y van seguidos del verbo base sin “to”.

- She can speak three languages. — _Ella sabe hablar tres idiomas._
- He should study more. — _Él debería estudiar más._

**Errores típicos**

- ✗ She cans swim. → ✔ She can swim.. Los modales no llevan -s.
- ✗ You should to rest. → ✔ You should rest.. Tras should no va “to”.
- ✗ Can you to help me? → ✔ Can you help me?. Tras can no va “to”.

**Quiz**

- “Deberías dormir más.”
  - ✔ You should sleep more.
  - ✗ You should to sleep more.
  - ✗ You should sleeping more.
- “Ella sabe cocinar.”
  - ✔ She can cook.
  - ✗ She cans cook.
  - ✗ She can to cook.
- “¿Podría ayudarme, por favor?”
  - ✔ Could you help me, please?
  - ✗ Could you to help me, please?
  - ✗ Could you helping me, please?
- “No debes fumar aquí.”
  - ✔ You mustn't smoke here.
  - ✗ You mustn't to smoke here.
  - ✗ You don't must smoke here.
