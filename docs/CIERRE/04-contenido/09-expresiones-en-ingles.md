# Biblioteca de expresiones en inglés (16)

Pantalla: `/chunks`. Tabla `chunks` (columnas: categoría, inglés, español, ejemplo, versión británica). Origen: `supabase/migrations/0001_init_schema.sql`. Se pueden agregar al repaso y se exportan a Anki desde Perfil.

## meetings (3)

- **Let's get started.** — Empecemos.
  - Ejemplo: Okay everyone, let's get started — we have a lot to cover today.
  - Británico: Right, shall we crack on?
- **The purpose of this meeting is to...** — El objetivo de esta reunión es...
  - Ejemplo: The purpose of this meeting is to review last month's sales numbers.
  - Británico: What we're here to sort out today is...
- **Just to bring everyone up to speed...** — Solo para poner a todos al día...
  - Ejemplo: Just to bring everyone up to speed, we closed two deals this week.
  - Británico: Just to fill you all in...

## opinion (2)

- **From my point of view...** — Desde mi punto de vista...
  - Ejemplo: From my point of view, the team needs more support on outbound calls.
  - Británico: The way I see it...
- **That's a fair point, but...** — Es un buen punto, pero...
  - Ejemplo: That's a fair point, but I don't think we have enough time this quarter.
  - Británico: Fair enough, but...

## clarification (2)

- **Could you run that by me again?** — ¿Podrías repetirme eso?
  - Ejemplo: Sorry, could you run that by me again? I want to make sure I understood.
  - Británico: Sorry, could you say that again?
- **Just to clarify, are you saying that...?** — Solo para aclarar, ¿estás diciendo que...?
  - Ejemplo: Just to clarify, are you saying that we need to close the deal by Friday?
  - Británico: So if I've got this right, you're saying...?

## email (3)

- **I hope this email finds you well.** — Espero que este correo te encuentre bien.
  - Ejemplo: Hi Sarah, I hope this email finds you well.
  - Británico: Hope you're keeping well.
- **I'm writing to follow up on...** — Te escribo para hacer seguimiento de...
  - Ejemplo: I'm writing to follow up on our conversation from last Tuesday.
  - Británico: I just wanted to chase up on...
- **Looking forward to hearing from you.** — Espero tu respuesta.
  - Ejemplo: Please let me know what works best. Looking forward to hearing from you.
  - Británico: Hope to hear from you soon.

## presentation (3)

- **What I'd like to show you today is...** — Lo que quiero mostrarles hoy es...
  - Ejemplo: What I'd like to show you today is our new outbound strategy.
  - Británico: What I want to walk you through today is...
- **The bottom line is...** — La conclusión es...
  - Ejemplo: The bottom line is, we increased sales by 20% this quarter.
  - Británico: At the end of the day...
- **Let me walk you through...** — Déjame guiarte por...
  - Ejemplo: Let me walk you through the numbers from last month.
  - Británico: Let me take you through...

## buytime (3)

- **That's a good question, let me think.** — Buena pregunta, déjame pensar.
  - Ejemplo: That's a good question, let me think about it for a second.
  - Británico: Good question — give me a moment.
- **What I mean is...** — Lo que quiero decir es...
  - Ejemplo: What I mean is, we need more time to close this properly.
  - Británico: What I'm getting at is...
- **Sorry, let me rephrase that.** — Perdón, déjame reformularlo.
  - Ejemplo: Sorry, let me rephrase that — what I'm trying to say is...
  - Británico: Let me put that another way.
