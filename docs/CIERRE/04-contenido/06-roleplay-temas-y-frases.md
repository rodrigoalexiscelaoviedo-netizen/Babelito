# Roleplay, temas de conversación y frases

## Escenas de roleplay (`/roleplay`)

### Inglés
- **Job interview** (B1) — You're interviewing for a role at an international company. El coach hace de _A friendly but direct HR manager at a British company_. Apertura: "Good morning, thanks for coming in. Could you start by telling me a bit about yourself?"
- **Team meeting** (B1) — You're leading a weekly meeting with your team in English. El coach hace de _A team member who's a bit confused about this week's goals_. Apertura: "Hi, before we start — can you clarify what the main goal is for this week?"
- **Client call** (B1) — You're on a call with a potential client interested in your services. El coach hace de _A curious potential client who asks lots of questions_. Apertura: "Hi! A colleague mentioned your company. Can you tell me what you offer exactly?"
- **Networking** (B1) — You're at an event and someone strikes up a conversation about what you do. El coach hace de _A relaxed, enthusiastic person at a social/professional event_. Apertura: "Hey! I don't think we've met — so what do you do?"
- **Presenting results** (B2) — You're presenting last quarter's results to a director. El coach hace de _A results-focused director who wants specific numbers and next steps_. Apertura: "Thanks for putting this together. Give me the highlights — what worked and what didn't?"
- **Small talk** (A2) — Casual conversation while waiting for a meeting to start. El coach hace de _A warm colleague making relaxed small talk_. Apertura: "Morning! Did you get up to anything nice over the weekend?"

## Tipos de error que detecta el coach

- `omitting_subject` — Sujeto omitido. En inglés el sujeto casi nunca se omite: 'Is possible' → 'It is possible'.
- `missing_to` — Falta 'to'. Tras need/want/like + verbo va 'to': 'I need learn' → 'I need to learn'.
- `missing_to_listen` — 'listen' + to. Se escucha algo CON 'to': 'I listen music' → 'I listen to music'.
- `adjective_plural` — Adjetivo en plural. Los adjetivos no pluralizan: 'differents' → 'different'.
- `false_friend` — Falso amigo. 'actually' = en realidad, no 'actualmente' (currently).
- `meal_verb` — Verbo con comidas. Las comidas se 'have': 'go to make breakfast' → 'have breakfast'.
- `word_order` — Orden de palabras. El inglés es bastante fijo: sujeto + verbo + objeto.
- `verb_tense` — Tiempo verbal. Ojo con present perfect vs past simple.
- `preposition` — Preposición. Las preposiciones rara vez se traducen literal del español.
- `article` — Artículos. Cuándo usar a/an/the y cuándo nada.
- `other` — Otro. Revisalo con el coach.
