## Job interview (B1)

Introduce yourself, talk about your experience and ask smart questions.

**Frases clave**

- Thank you for having me. — _Gracias por recibirme._
- I have three years of experience in sales. — _Tengo tres años de experiencia en ventas._
- My greatest strength is staying calm under pressure. — _Mi mayor fortaleza es mantener la calma bajo presión._
- I'm looking for a role where I can grow. — _Busco un puesto donde pueda crecer._
- In my previous job, I was responsible for the website. — _En mi trabajo anterior, era responsable del sitio web._
- Could you tell me more about the team? — _¿Podría contarme más sobre el equipo?_
- I'm available to start in two weeks. — _Estoy disponible para empezar en dos semanas._
- What are the next steps in the process? — _¿Cuáles son los siguientes pasos del proceso?_

**Vocabulario**

- strength — _fortaleza_
- weakness — _debilidad_
- deadline — _fecha límite_
- salary — _salario_
- skills — _habilidades_
- reference — _referencia (recomendación)_

**Diálogo modelo** (el otro personaje: Interviewer)

- **Interviewer:** Thanks for coming in. Tell me a bit about yourself.
- **Tú:** Thank you for having me. I'm a marketing assistant with three years of experience.
- **Interviewer:** What's your biggest strength?
- **Tú:** I work well in a team, and I stay calm under pressure.
- **Interviewer:** And a weakness?
- **Tú:** Sometimes I take on too much work, but I'm learning to prioritise.
- **Interviewer:** Do you have any questions for us?
- **Tú:** Yes. What are the next steps in the process?

**Quiz**

- Te piden: “Tell me about yourself.” ¿Qué respuesta es la mejor?
  - ✔ I'm a marketing assistant with three years of experience.
  - ✗ I am born in 1995 and I like pizza.
  - ✗ Myself is Ana and I work.
- Quieres decir que tienes tres años de experiencia en ventas.
  - ✔ I have three years of experience in sales.
  - ✗ I am three years of experience in sales.
  - ✗ I have experience of sales since three years.
- Al final quieres saber qué viene después.
  - ✔ What are the next steps in the process?
  - ✗ What is next step the process?
  - ✗ Which is the following of the process?
- Agradeces al entrar a la entrevista.
  - ✔ Thank you for having me.
  - ✗ Thanks for have me.
  - ✗ Thank you for I am here.

**Roleplay** — el coach hace de _A friendly but direct HR manager interviewing the user for a marketing job._. A full interview: introductions, strengths and your own questions.
Apertura: "Good morning, and thanks for coming in. Could you start by telling me a bit about yourself?"

## Work meetings (B2)

Lead a discussion, disagree politely, clarify and close with clear next steps.

**Frases clave**

- Let's get started. — _Empecemos._
- Could you clarify what you mean by that? — _¿Podrías aclarar a qué te refieres?_
- I see your point, but I'd suggest a different approach. — _Entiendo tu punto, pero sugeriría otro enfoque._
- Let me share my screen. — _Déjame compartir mi pantalla._
- Can we circle back to that later? — _¿Podemos volver a eso más tarde?_
- I'll follow up with an email after the meeting. — _Haré un seguimiento por email después de la reunión._
- We're running out of time, so let's wrap up. — _Se nos acaba el tiempo, así que cerremos._
- Who's going to take ownership of this? — _¿Quién se hará cargo de esto?_

**Vocabulario**

- agenda — _orden del día_
- deadline — _fecha límite_
- stakeholder — _parte interesada_
- action item — _tarea pendiente_
- update — _novedad / actualización_
- budget — _presupuesto_

**Diálogo modelo** (el otro personaje: Colleague)

- **Tú:** Let's get started. The first item on the agenda is the launch date.
- **Colleague:** I think we need two more weeks. The design isn't ready.
- **Tú:** I see your point, but I'd suggest a different approach. Could we launch a smaller version first?
- **Colleague:** That could work. What would it include?
- **Tú:** Only the main features. Can we circle back to the details later?
- **Colleague:** Sure. Who's going to take ownership of the plan?
- **Tú:** I will. I'll follow up with an email after the meeting.
- **Colleague:** Perfect. Thanks, everyone.

**Quiz**

- Abres la reunión.
  - ✔ Let's get started.
  - ✗ Start we now.
  - ✗ We go to begin the meeting.
- Pides que te aclaren algo.
